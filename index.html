2. Helmet / mask pieces

Pieces should be exported in the same coordinates (as assembled). You can also drag files onto the 3D view.

    How Groups & Auto-assemble work

    Click + New group and click-and-drag pieces onto a group folder (like Photoshop / Illustrator layers) to organize pre-assembled sections, or drag pieces out to ungroup.

    • All groups stay together and do not get disassembled; unlocked groups can be resized as a whole.
    • Locking preserves coordinate status relative to other locked pieces and size.
    • Locked groups do not get resized or disassociated.
    • Different groups are assembled together with other groups — each group acts like a pre-assembled section of a puzzle being fitted to other sections.
    • Single items not part of a group or locked follow the same rules as before.
    • Hidden items or groups do not participate in Auto-assemble.

    Pieces laid out for printing can be put back together with Auto-assemble (needs flat cuts or connectors). Use Snap for anything it gets wrong or can't place, then Auto-assemble again to fill in the rest around your joins.
